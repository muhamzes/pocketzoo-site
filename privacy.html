Privacy Policy

Pocket Zoo is a personal, single-user automation project. It has no public users, does not offer any service, product, or login to third parties, and does not collect, store, or share any personal data belonging to anyone other than its owner.

The only external access this project uses is its own YouTube channel's upload permission (via the YouTube Data API), used solely to publish pre-generated videos to the owner's own channel on an automated schedule. No data from any other Google account, YouTube user, or viewer is accessed, collected, or shared.

This policy may be updated from time to time. Last updated: September 2026.
